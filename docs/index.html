Phittoos is an Android app for keeping track of money lent to or borrowed from friends.

Key Features (V1)

  • Offline-First Design: All your financial and peer records are kept fully locally in a secure on-device SQLite database. No automatic network synchronization.
  • Lent & Borrowed Tracking: Record exact amounts, categories, custom descriptions, and transaction directions effortlessly.
  • Due Dates & Indicators: Assign precise due dates to transactions with clear color-coded statuses (upcoming, due, or overdue).
  • Partial & Full Repayments: Record multiple partial repayments over time, keeping an exact, updated history of remaining balances.
  • Settlements: Fully settle active ledgers with a single tap, cleanly closing outstanding debts.
  • On-Device Reminders: Local background alerts scheduled using WorkManager alert you to overdue transactions without relying on any external notification servers.
  • User-Controlled Backups: Securely export and restore complete database backups in structured JSON, or export your ledger as a CSV file using Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF).
